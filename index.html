Meet Halo

For those moments, we made Halo.

Halo is a small, discreet device that attaches to your laptop. If someone lifts or moves your laptop while you’re away, an 80 dB alarm goes off.

The alarm does two things: it alerts you, and it puts the spotlight on the thief. People nearby hear it and notice, so Halo itself works as a deterrent.
